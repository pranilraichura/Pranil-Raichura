# Activity mark sources

These are real institution or project marks, imported on 2026-08-02. Raster files were resized or losslessly re-encoded for web delivery; their artwork was not redrawn.

- `mit.webp` — [MIT Brand Guide](https://brand.mit.edu/logos-marks/mit-logo), MIT-red logo asset.
- `johns-hopkins.svg` — Johns Hopkins University primary logo, matched against the [official Johns Hopkins brand guide](https://brand.jhu.edu/visual-identity/primary-logo/) and downloaded from the university logo file hosted by Wikimedia.
- `stanford.webp` — [Stanford Identity Guide](https://identity.stanford.edu/visual-identity/stanford-logos/), Block S with tree.
- `ucla.svg` — [UCLA Brand Guidelines](https://brand.ucla.edu/identity/logos-and-marks/campus-logo), official campus logo. The supplied white artwork uses the official UCLA Blue color for this light-background placement.
- `usc.webp` — [USC Brand and Identity Guidelines](https://identity.usc.edu/identity/logos-marks/), primary logotype.
- `ieee.svg` — IEEE master logo, verified against the [IEEE Brand Experience site](https://brand-experience.ieee.org/ieee-brand/brand-overview/) and downloaded from its Wikimedia-hosted logo file.
- `nasa.webp` — [NASA insignia asset](https://heasarc.gsfc.nasa.gov/docs/tess/images/logos/) hosted by NASA's HEASARC.
- `clearpolicy.webp` — live ClearPolicy logo from [clearpolicy.org](https://clearpolicy.org/).
- `flora-frontier.webp` — live project artwork from [Flora Frontier](https://pranil.itch.io/flora-frontier).
